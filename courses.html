Common Questions

Which course should I start with?
If you are completely new to the industry, I highly recommend starting with the "AI-Driven Full Digital Marketing" course to build a strong foundation across all channels. If you already have experience and want to specialize, choose the specific path (like Meta Ads or Server-Side Tracking) that matches your career goal.
Do I need previous digital marketing experience?
No previous experience is required for the beginner-friendly courses like Website SEO or Local SEO. However, advanced modules like "Server-Side Tracking" require a basic understanding of how pixels and website analytics function.
Is the training practical?
Absolutely. The core philosophy of my training is "Understand → Practice → Implement." We do not rely on just theory. You will work inside the actual tools (GA4, GTM, Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads) and execute strategies based on real-world scenarios.
How long is each course and what is the format?
Course duration varies depending on the topic. Specialized courses can be completed in a few weeks, while the comprehensive Masterclass is a multi-month journey. Click "Enroll Now" on any course to see the detailed curriculum, class schedule, and format (Live or Pre-recorded).
How does payment and enrollment work?
When you click on "Enroll Now," you will be redirected to the secure external course landing page. There you can view all available payment gateways, pricing plans, and complete your registration seamlessly.
How do I access the course after enrollment?
Upon successful enrollment, you will receive an immediate confirmation email containing your login credentials and instructions on how to access the learning portal, community groups, and class links.
Is this suitable for freelancers or business owners?
Both! Freelancers will learn the exact execution skills needed to offer high-paying services on Upwork and Fiverr. Business owners will learn the strategy and metrics needed to oversee their own growth and stop relying blindly on agencies.
