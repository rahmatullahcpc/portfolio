Meta Ads & Lead Gen

Generating 500+ High-Quality Leads for a Property Developer

Deployed a full-funnel Facebook and Instagram ad campaign utilizing custom audiences to generate high-quality, conversion-ready property leads.

Client Industry

Property Developer

Services

Meta Ads Lead Generation

Platform

Facebook & Instagram

Key Focus

Lowering CPA & Lead Quality

The Challenge

The client was running Facebook ads but generating "junk" leads. Their sales team was frustrated because the prospects were unqualified, didn't answer calls, or had no actual budget. This resulted in a huge waste of ad spend and valuable sales team time following up with low-intent users.

The Strategy

We moved away from generic lead strategies and built a highly targeted Conversion Funnel designed to filter out unqualified audiences:

  • Funnel Shift: Switched from standard on-Facebook Lead Forms to a Conversion-objective landing page funnel to add friction and filter out low-intent users.
  • Creative Refresh: Replaced static images with highly engaging, fast-paced video tours of the properties to capture attention and build desire.
  • Advanced Targeting: Uploaded the client's CRM data of past buyers to create 1% Lookalike (LAL) audiences, targeting users with similar purchasing behavior.
  • Lead Qualification: Set up a multi-step form with strict qualifying questions (e.g., budget range, timeframe to buy) to score leads before they ever reached the sales team.

The Results

Within two months, the funnel stabilized. Not only did we reduce the cost per lead, but the actual quality of the leads drastically improved the sales team's closing rate.
