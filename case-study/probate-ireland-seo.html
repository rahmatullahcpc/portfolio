SEO & Local SEO

150% Increase in Organic Traffic for Probate Ireland

Improving local search visibility and organic lead acquisition through technical SEO and Google Business Profile strategy.

Client Industry

Law Firm / Legal Services

Services

Technical & On-Page SEO

Platform

WordPress

Key Focus

Local Search Dominance

The Challenge

Probate Ireland had a professional website but was virtually invisible on Google for high-intent legal queries. They were missing out on valuable local searches (e.g., "probate solicitors near me") and were relying entirely on expensive paid advertising and traditional referrals to acquire new clients.

The Strategy

We executed a robust, multi-layered SEO strategy targeting long-tail commercial keywords and local map pack optimization:

  • Technical SEO Audit & Fixes: Resolved critical crawl errors, improved Core Web Vitals (site speed), and fixed broken internal links that were hurting indexability.
  • On-Page Optimization: Rewrote meta titles, descriptions, and H1/H2 tags for core service pages to align with user search intent.
  • Google Business Profile (GBP): Completely optimized their GBP profile with accurate NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data, Q&A sections, and geo-tagged images.
  • Content Mapping: Created highly relevant pillar pages answering common legal questions to build topical authority.

The Results

Over a 6-month period, the organic foundation we built completely transformed their lead generation pipeline, drastically reducing their reliance on paid ads.
