SEO & Local SEO

Scaling an Ireland-Based B2B Brand to Page 1 Globally

Comprehensive Website SEO audit, content mapping, and optimization leading to Page 1 rankings for highly competitive commercial keywords.

Client Industry

Ireland-based B2B Client

Services

Technical SEO & Content Strategy

Platform

Custom Web Platform

Key Focus

Commercial Keyword Ranking

The Challenge

The client had a strong foundational business in Ireland but struggled to gain visibility in broader European and UK markets. Their website suffered from severe keyword cannibalization, messy URL structures, and lacked the topical authority needed to compete against larger multinational corporations for high-value B2B commercial keywords.

The Strategy

We launched a full-scale Technical and Content SEO overhaul designed to establish them as the absolute authority in their niche:

  • Deep Technical Audit: Resolved complex crawlability issues, fixed Core Web Vitals, and properly implemented international `hreflang` tags for regional targeting.
  • Content Mapping & Pruning: Restructured the entire site architecture. We consolidated overlapping pages to eliminate keyword cannibalization and created distinct pillar pages for their core B2B services.
  • Answer Engine Optimization (AEO): Optimized their high-level service pages to capture Featured Snippets ("Position Zero") on Google for informational queries made by decision-makers.
  • Internal Linking Strategy: Built a logical, siloed internal linking structure to flow "link juice" directly to their most profitable commercial landing pages.

The Results

By executing a strictly data-driven SEO framework, we transformed their website from a local brochure into a global B2B lead generation asset.
