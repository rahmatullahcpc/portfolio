Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really 100% free?
Yes! The hosting plans recommended here are completely free for students and beginners. You don't need a credit card to sign up.
Can I practice GTM and Pixel tracking here?
Absolutely. Since you get full access to the WordPress dashboard and theme files, you can easily insert Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, and practice conversion tracking.
Can I use my own domain name?
Yes, you can buy a domain from Namecheap or GoDaddy and point the nameservers to this free hosting account to make it look completely professional.
Is this hosting good for a real client business?
No. Free hosting is meant for practice, portfolios, and testing. For a real business or e-commerce store, I always recommend purchasing a premium, high-speed hosting provider.
